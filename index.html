Guided treks

Hiking

As experienced mountain guides, we are passionate about showing you the raw beauty of Georgia's grandest landscapes. From soaring alpine peaks and dramatic glaciers to ancient valleys, we lead you safely through nature's finest trails while sharing the rich history, culture, and warm hospitality that make Georgia unforgettable.

Mountain region

Tours in Svaneti

Step into Georgia's high-mountain wonderland with our guided Svaneti tours. Home to the iconic UNESCO-listed Svan towers and breathtaking views of Mount Shkhara, Svaneti blends pristine nature with ancient tradition. Whether exploring the historic streets of Mestia, trekking to alpine lakes, or visiting Ushguli — one of Europe's highest inhabited villages — we craft unforgettable mountain adventures tailored to you.

Outdoors

Camping tours

Tents by alpine lakes in Tusheti, Kazbegi and Lagodekhi. We handle permits, transport, gear and food — you carry a day pack and show up. Weekend and week-long formats, small groups, and a guide who knows which valleys still have no phone signal.

Ski & snowboard instructors
